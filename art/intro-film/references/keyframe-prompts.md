# Darkroom — keyframe prompts for ChatGPT Images

Use 16:9 (1920×1080) for all three. Paste the shared style block after each prompt.

## Shared style block

> Cinematic 35mm film still, spherical 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, fine organic film grain, soft halation around the brightest highlights, gentle vignette. Photographic and tactile — real paper, real liquid, real steel. Not CGI, not a 3D render, not pixel art, no text, no logos, no watermark. The darks are a warm, deep oxblood (#1d0b0a), never flat pure black.

---

## Frame 1: Safelight

> A photographic darkroom lit only by a deep crimson safelight. Low camera over a stainless-steel sink, looking into a shallow white developing tray filled with clear developer. A blank sheet of photographic paper lies just under the liquid surface; slow concentric ripples spread from where bamboo print tongs with red rubber tips touch the liquid. Behind, out of focus: a line of black-and-white prints hanging from wooden clothespins, amber chemical bottles catching red highlights, and a round safelight lamp glowing as a soft red disc. On the left, a vintage darkroom timer whose luminous dial glows a faint teal — the only cool color in the frame. Water droplets on the steel reflect the red light. Quiet, expectant, the moment before an image appears.

## Frame 2: Developing (attach a photo, e.g. `football-action.webp`)

> Close macro shot looking down into a developing tray under a red safelight. Beneath a thin layer of clear developer, a black-and-white photograph is halfway through developing: the attached photo is emerging on the paper. The deepest shadows are already dense, the midtones are still faint and ghostly, the highlights are still blank paper. A soft, rippled reflection of the red safelight slides across one corner of the liquid surface; tiny ripples bend the image beneath. The tip of bamboo tongs enters the frame at the top right. Focus falls on the face; everything else melts into red bokeh.

## Frame 3: Lights on (attach the portrait `tim.jpg`)

> The white light has just come on in the darkroom. A finished black-and-white silver-gelatin print of the attached portrait (a side profile) hangs from a line by two wooden clothespins at its top corners, still wet, with a slight sheen and a single droplet at the bottom edge. The print sits on the right side of the frame. The left 45% of the frame is dark, soft negative space, with another print hanging blurred far behind. A single warm-white work light rakes in from the upper left, and the light falls off quickly into a deep warm charcoal background. A faint trace of red safelight still glows at the right edge. Palette: warm paper white (#eae7df), ink black (#111210), the red now only a memory.

### Notes
- For consistency, generate Frame 1 first, then attach it to prompts 2 and 3 as a style reference ("match the lighting, grain and color of the attached still").
- Frame 3 is the hand-off frame. The portrait's position and scale should match the site's hero, where the particle portrait sits right of centre facing right, so the film can cut straight into it.
